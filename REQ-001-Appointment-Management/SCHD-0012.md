# SCHD-0012: Student edits a pending appointment (change session mode)

**Summary:** Verify that a student can switch the session format between online and in-person on a pending appointment.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **student** account
- The student has a **pending** online appointment and a **pending** in-person appointment

**Scenario 1: Change session mode between online and in-person**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Student clicks "Edit" on a pending online appointment | Verify that the booking form loads with "Google Meet" pre-selected |
| 2. Student selects "In-person" in the session format section | Verify that "In-person" is highlighted and "Main Campus Lounge" is shown |
| 3. Student clicks "Save Changes" | Verify that the submission succeeds |
| 4. Student opens the appointment detail | Verify that the mode is displayed as "In-person" and no Google Meet link is shown |
| 5. Student clicks "Edit" on a pending in-person appointment | Verify that the booking form loads with "In-person" pre-selected |
| 6. Student selects "Google Meet" in the session format section | Verify that "Google Meet" is highlighted and "Secure video call link" is shown |
| 7. Student clicks "Save Changes" | Verify that the submission succeeds |
| 8. Student opens the appointment detail | Verify that the mode is displayed as "Online" |

**Post-conditions:**
- The mode is updated on both appointments
- No Google Meet link is generated at this stage (only on counselor approval)
