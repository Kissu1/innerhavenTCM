# SCHD-0020: Counselor completes a past approved appointment

**Summary:** Verify that a counselor can mark a past approved appointment as completed.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **counselor** account
- A student has an **approved** appointment with this counselor where the appointment date is in the past

**Scenario 1: Complete a past approved appointment**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Counselor navigates to the appointments page or detail page for a past approved appointment | Verify that the appointment card shows a "Mark Complete" action (in dropdown or detail page) |
| 2. Counselor clicks "Mark Complete" | Verify that a loading state appears |
| 3. The request completes | Verify that the appointment card's status badge changes to **"Completed"** |
| 4. Counselor opens the appointment detail | Verify that the status is shown as "Completed" and a "Session Feedback" option is now available |

**Post-conditions:**
- The appointment status is "Completed"
- The counselor can now create session notes/feedback
