# SCHD-0004: Student attempts to book a past date

**Summary:** Verify that the system prevents booking appointments for dates in the past.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **student** account
- The student navigates to the booking page

**Scenario 1: Select a date in the past**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Student selects a counselor | Verify that the counselor profile card is shown |
| 2. Student navigates the calendar to a past month and selects a past date | Verify that no available time slots are shown, or past time slots appear dimmed/disabled and cannot be selected |
