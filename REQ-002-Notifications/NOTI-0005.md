# NOTI-0005: Student clicks a notification and navigates to the related content

**Summary:** Verify that clicking a notification card navigates the student to the related appointment or messaging thread.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **student** account
- The student has notifications linked to appointments (e.g., booking_approved, session_notes)

**Scenario 1: Click a booking notification**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Student views a notification for an appointment event (e.g., "Your booking for June 15 at 10:00 has been approved") | Verify that the notification card is displayed and clickable |
| 2. Student clicks the notification card | Verify that the student is taken directly to the appointment detail page for that specific appointment |
| 3. Verify the destination | Verify that the appointment detail matches the appointment referenced in the notification |

**Post-conditions:**
- The student is taken to the relevant content for the notification
