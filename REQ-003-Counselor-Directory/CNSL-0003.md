# CNSL-0003: Student browses the booking calendar across multiple weeks

**Summary:** Verify that when a student opens the booking calendar for a counselor, they can navigate across weeks and see which dates have available slots, which are fully booked, and which are off-days.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **student** account
- A counselor exists with a Mon–Fri schedule
- One appointment is already booked on the first Monday of the displayed range

**Scenario 1: Browse calendar month view and identify available dates**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Student navigates to the booking page and selects the counselor | Verify that a month-view calendar appears |
| 2. Student looks at the current week (Mon–Fri) | Verify that dates within the counselor's schedule show an indicator of available slots |
| 3. Student looks at the first Monday (which has a booked appointment) | Verify that the date still shows as having slots — but when clicked, the booked slot is missing from the time grid |
| 4. Student looks at Saturday and Sunday | Verify that weekend dates appear disabled, grayed out, or show no availability indicator |
| 5. Student navigates to the next week using the calendar controls | Verify that the next week's dates load and show availability based on the counselor's recurring schedule |
| 6. Student attempts to navigate beyond 6 weeks ahead | Verify that dates beyond 42 days from today are disabled or not shown |

**Post-conditions:**
- The calendar correctly reflects the counselor's schedule across multiple weeks
- Weekends and off-days are visually distinct from available dates
- The student cannot look up availability more than 42 days into the future
