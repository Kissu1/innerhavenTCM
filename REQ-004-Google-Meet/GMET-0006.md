# GMET-0006: Counselor disconnects Google account

**Summary:** Verify that a counselor can disconnect their Google account from the settings page, after which online appointment approvals will require reconnection.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **counselor** account who has Google connected

**Scenario 1: Disconnect Google and verify the change**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Counselor navigates to the settings page | Verify that the Google integration section shows "Connected" with a "Disconnect" button |
| 2. Counselor clicks "Disconnect Google Account" | Verify that the settings page updates to show "Connect Google Account" instead — the disconnect is immediate |
| 3. Counselor navigates to a pending online appointment and tries to approve it | Verify that the approval is blocked with a prompt to reconnect Google (see GMET-0004), confirming the disconnection took effect |
| 4. Counselor returns to settings and clicks "Connect Google Account" | Verify that they can go through the Google connection flow again and reconnect successfully |

**Post-conditions:**
- The counselor's Google connection is fully removed
- Approving online appointments now requires reconnection
- The counselor can reconnect at any time
