# ANON-0007: Student views their list of anonymous conversations

**Summary:** Verify that a student can see all their anonymous conversations, identify each by the counselor's name, and open any conversation to continue messaging.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **student** account
- The student has active anonymous conversations with two different counselors
- The student also has one closed conversation with a third counselor

**Scenario 1: View and manage personal conversation list**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Student navigates to the messaging page | Verify that a list of their conversations is displayed |
| 2. Student scans the conversation list | Verify that each active conversation shows: the counselor's name and avatar, a preview of the last message, and how long ago the last activity was |
| 3. Student identifies the two active conversations | Verify that both active conversations with different counselors are present and distinguishable |
| 4. Student clicks on one of the active conversations | Verify that the full conversation opens and the student can continue messaging |
| 5. Student returns to the list and checks for the closed conversation | Verify that the closed conversation is either shown in a separate section (e.g., "Closed") or marked as closed — it is distinguishable from active conversations |
| 6. Student checks the "New Conversation" option | Verify that the student can still start a new conversation with a different counselor if needed |

**Post-conditions:**
- The student sees all their conversations — both active and closed
- Active conversations are clearly distinguishable from closed ones
- The student can open any conversation to view its history
- The counselor's identity is always visible to the student in their conversation list
