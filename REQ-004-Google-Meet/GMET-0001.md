# GMET-0001: Counselor connects Google account (happy path)

**Summary:** Verify that a counselor can connect their Google account from the settings page to enable Google Meet link generation for online appointments.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **counselor** account
- The counselor has **not** previously connected a Google account
- Google OAuth is configured on the server

**Scenario 1: Complete Google account connection**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Counselor navigates to the settings page and clicks "Connect Google Account" | Verify that the browser is redirected to Google's consent screen asking for permission to create Meet sessions |
| 2. Counselor grants permission on the Google consent screen | Verify that Google redirects back to the app and the counselor's account is linked |
| 3. The counselor is returned to the dashboard | Verify that a success confirmation is shown (e.g., a banner or notification: "Google account connected") |
| 4. Counselor returns to the settings page | Verify that the Google section now shows "Connected" with a "Disconnect" button instead of "Connect" |

**Post-conditions:**
- The counselor's Google account is securely linked to their profile
- The settings page reflects the connected status
- The counselor can now approve online appointments to generate Meet links
