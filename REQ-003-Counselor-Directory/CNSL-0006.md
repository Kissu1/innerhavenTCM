# CNSL-0006: Counselor views their own schedule and upcoming appointments

**Summary:** Verify that a counselor can view their weekly availability schedule and see upcoming appointments booked against their slots.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **counselor** account
- The counselor has set a weekly schedule (e.g., Mon–Fri, 9:00–17:00)
- At least two students have booked appointments with this counselor on different days

**Scenario 1: Counselor reviews their schedule with booked appointments**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Counselor navigates to their dashboard or schedule page | Verify that the counselor's weekly schedule is displayed showing their working days and time blocks |
| 2. Counselor looks at a day with booked appointments | Verify that each booked appointment appears on that day showing the student's name, time, and status (e.g., "Pending" or "Approved") |
| 3. Counselor looks at a day with no appointments | Verify that the day shows the counselor's available time blocks but no student bookings |
| 4. Counselor looks at a weekend or off-day | Verify that the day is shown as unavailable — no schedule blocks are displayed |
| 5. Counselor confirms they only see their own appointments | Verify that no appointments belonging to other counselors appear in this view |

**Post-conditions:**
- The counselor sees only their own schedule and appointments
- Booked slots are visually distinct from open slots
- Off-days are clearly marked as unavailable
