# CNSL-0007: Counselor updates their weekly availability schedule

**Summary:** Verify that a counselor can set or change their weekly working hours and days, and that the updated schedule is reflected when students check availability.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **counselor** account
- The counselor currently has no schedule set, or has an existing schedule

**Scenario 1: Set and update weekly availability**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Counselor navigates to their settings or schedule page | Verify that a weekly schedule editor is displayed with toggles for each day of the week and time range inputs |
| 2. Counselor enables Monday through Friday and sets hours to 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM with a 50-minute session duration | Verify that the selections are saved and a confirmation message appears |
| 3. Counselor checks that Saturday and Sunday are disabled | Verify that the weekend days are toggled off and show no working hours |
| 4. A student navigates to the booking page, selects this counselor, and checks a Monday date | Verify that time slots appear matching the counselor's newly set schedule (e.g., 9:00, 9:50, 10:40, …) |
| 5. The student checks a Saturday date | Verify that no time slots are available — the counselor does not work weekends |
| 6. Counselor returns to settings and changes Friday to end at 3:00 PM instead of 5:00 PM | Verify that the change is saved |
| 7. The student checks a Friday date again | Verify that the last available slot now ends around 3:00 PM, reflecting the counselor's updated schedule |

**Post-conditions:**
- The counselor's schedule is stored and immediately reflected in student availability queries
- Changes to the schedule take effect right away — no caching delay for new dates
- Students see accurate availability based on the counselor's current schedule
