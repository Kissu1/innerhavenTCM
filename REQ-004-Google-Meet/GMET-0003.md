# GMET-0003: No Meet link generated for in-person appointment approval

**Summary:** Verify that when a counselor approves an in-person appointment, no Google Meet link is created — the appointment simply shows the office location instead.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **counselor** account who has Google connected
- A pending **in-person** appointment exists for this counselor

**Scenario 1: Approve in-person appointment without Meet link**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Counselor opens the pending in-person appointment | Verify that the appointment shows "in-person" and a pending status |
| 2. Counselor clicks "Approve" | Verify that the appointment updates successfully and the status changes to "Approved" |
| 3. Counselor inspects the approved appointment | Verify that no "Join Google Meet" link or video call button appears — instead the office location or room number is displayed |
| 4. The student associated with this appointment logs in and views it | Verify that the student also sees the office location, not a Meet link |

**Post-conditions:**
- The appointment is approved with no Google Meet link
- The office location is displayed for both the counselor and the student
- Only online appointments produce Meet links
