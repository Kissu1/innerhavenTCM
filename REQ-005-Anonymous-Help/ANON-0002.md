# ANON-0002: Student and counselor exchange messages in an active conversation

**Summary:** Verify that once an anonymous conversation is started, both the student and the counselor can send and receive messages in real time, and the student's identity remains hidden from the counselor.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **student** account who has an active anonymous conversation with a counselor
- The counselor is also available and viewing the conversation

**Scenario 1: Back-and-forth messaging in an active conversation**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Student opens their active conversation and types a follow-up message: "It's been happening mostly at night" | Verify that the message appears immediately in the chat with a sending indicator, then shows as delivered |
| 2. Counselor sees the new message appear in the same conversation | Verify that the message content is visible to the counselor, but the sender is still labeled with the anonymous label (e.g., "ANON-A1B2") |
| 3. Counselor types a reply: "Thank you for sharing that. Can you tell me more about what happens at night?" | Verify that the counselor's message appears in the conversation with their name and avatar |
| 4. Student sees the counselor's reply | Verify that the message appears in the student's chat view showing the counselor's name and avatar |
| 5. Student sends another message with more details | Verify that the conversation history shows all messages in chronological order, with clear distinction between student and counselor messages |
| 6. Both parties scroll back through the conversation | Verify that all previous messages are preserved and readable |

**Post-conditions:**
- All messages in the conversation are preserved and visible to both parties
- The student's identity remains hidden from the counselor throughout (only the anonymous label is shown)
- The counselor's identity is visible to the student
- Messages appear in correct chronological order
