# SCHD-0013: Student attempts to edit a pending appointment with a taken timeslot

**Summary:** Verify that when editing a pending appointment, the student cannot select a timeslot that is already booked by someone else.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **student** account
- The student has a **pending** appointment
- Another student has booked a timeslot at the target date/time for the same counselor

**Scenario 1: Taken slot is unavailable during edit**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Student clicks "Edit" on a pending appointment | Verify that the booking form loads |
| 2. Student changes the date to a date where some slots are taken by other students | Verify that the taken slots appear as unavailable/disabled in the time slot grid |
| 3. Student attempts to click on a taken slot | Verify that the slot cannot be selected |
