# GMET-0004: Counselor prompted to reconnect Google when connection expires

**Summary:** Verify that when a counselor's Google connection has expired and they try to approve an online appointment, they are prompted to reconnect their Google account instead of the approval silently failing.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **counselor** account
- The counselor previously connected Google, but the connection has since expired (e.g., token was revoked via Google account settings)
- A pending **online** appointment exists for this counselor

**Scenario 1: Approval blocked — counselor sees reconnect prompt**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Counselor opens the pending online appointment and clicks "Approve" | Verify that instead of approving, a message appears: "Your Google account connection has expired. Please reconnect to approve online sessions." |
| 2. The message includes a call to action | Verify that a "Reconnect Google Account" button or link is shown alongside the message |
| 3. Counselor checks the appointment status | Verify that the appointment is still "Pending" — it was not approved and no Meet link was created |
| 4. Counselor clicks "Reconnect Google Account" and completes the OAuth flow (see GMET-0001) | Verify that after reconnecting, the counselor returns and can now approve the appointment successfully |

**Post-conditions:**
- The appointment remains pending until Google is reconnected
- The counselor is guided to reconnect rather than seeing a cryptic error
- After reconnection, normal approval flow resumes
