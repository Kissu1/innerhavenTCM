# NOTI-0013: Notifications update in real time without page refresh

**Summary:** Verify that when a new notification is generated (e.g., a booking request), it appears in the bell dropdown and the badge updates without requiring a page refresh.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **counselor** account
- The counselor has the page open with the navbar visible
- The bell currently shows no unread badge (or a known count)

**Scenario 1: Real-time notification arrival**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. A student books an appointment with this counselor (from a different browser/session) | Verify that the red badge on the bell icon appears or increments without the counselor refreshing the page |
| 2. Counselor clicks the bell icon | Verify that the new notification is visible in the dropdown with the correct message and timestamp |

**Post-conditions:**
- The notification is delivered in real time to the counselor
