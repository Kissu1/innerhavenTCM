# NOTI-0002: Student opens the notification bell dropdown

**Summary:** Verify that clicking the notification bell opens a dropdown showing the most recent 5 notifications with their message and timestamp.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **student** account
- At least one notification exists for the student

**Scenario 1: Open the dropdown and view recent notifications**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Student clicks the bell icon in the navbar | Verify that a dropdown panel opens showing recent notifications |
| 2. Verify the notification items | Verify that the most recent notifications are listed, each showing the notification message and when it was received (e.g., "2 hours ago", "Yesterday") |
| 3. Verify the unread visual distinction | Verify that unread notifications stand out visually — they appear bolder and more prominent than read ones |
| 4. Verify the read visual distinction | Verify that read notifications appear dimmed and less prominent than unread ones |
| 5. Scroll to the bottom of the dropdown | Verify that a "View all notifications" link is displayed |
| 6. Student clicks "View all notifications" | Verify that the student is taken to the full notifications page |

**Post-conditions:**
- The student can see their most recent notifications at a glance without leaving the current page
