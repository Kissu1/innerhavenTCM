# SCHD-0005: Student attempts to book an already-taken timeslot

**Summary:** Verify that the system prevents double-booking by disabling taken slots in the time slot grid.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **student** account (Student A)
- Another student (Student B) has already booked an appointment with a specific counselor on a specific date and time
- Student A navigates to the booking page

**Scenario 1: Taken slot is unavailable in the grid**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Student A selects the same counselor that Student B booked with | Verify that the counselor profile card is shown |
| 2. Student A selects the same date that Student B booked | Verify that the time slots are displayed |
| 3. Student A looks for the time slot that Student B booked | Verify that the slot appears as unavailable/disabled and cannot be selected |

**Post-conditions:**
- No duplicate booking is created
- The original booking remains unchanged
