# SCHD-0017: Counselor approves a pending online appointment

**Summary:** Verify that when a counselor approves an online appointment, the system generates a Google Meet link, and both the counselor and student can see and use it.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **counselor** account
- A student has a **pending** **online** appointment with this counselor
- The counselor has a valid Google connection (Google token is active)

**Scenario 1: Approve online appointment and verify meeting link**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Counselor navigates to the appointments page and finds a pending online appointment | Verify that the appointment card shows an "Accept" action in the dropdown menu |
| 2. Counselor clicks "Accept" | Verify that a loading state appears (e.g., "Accepting..." text) |
| 3. The request completes | Verify that the appointment card's status badge changes to **"Approved"** with a blue/tertiary color |
| 4. Counselor opens the appointment detail | Verify that a Google Meet link is displayed in the meeting logistics section |
| 5. Verify the meeting link is valid | Verify that the meeting link opens a real Google Meet session when clicked |
| 6. The student who booked the appointment checks their appointments page | Verify that the appointment now shows status **"Approved"** |
| 7. Student opens the appointment detail | Verify that a "Join Session Now" button or Google Meet link is displayed |
| 8. Student clicks "Join Session Now" | Verify that Google Meet opens with the session ready to join |

**Post-conditions:**
- The appointment status is "Approved"
- A Google Meet link is generated and stored
- Both student and counselor can see and access the meeting link
