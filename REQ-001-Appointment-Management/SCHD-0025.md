# SCHD-0025: Student cannot edit an approved or completed appointment

**Summary:** Verify that once a counselor has approved an appointment (or marked it as completed), the student can no longer edit the appointment details.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **student** account
- The student has one **approved** appointment and one **completed** appointment

**Scenario 1: Edit options are unavailable for approved and completed appointments**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Student navigates to the appointments page and finds the approved appointment | Verify that the appointment card shows status "Approved" |
| 2. Student checks the actions available on the approved appointment | Verify that the "Edit" option is **not** available — only "Cancel" and "View Details" are shown |
| 3. Student opens the approved appointment detail | Verify that there is no "Edit" button or link anywhere on the detail page |
| 4. Student navigates back and finds the completed appointment | Verify that the appointment card shows status "Completed" |
| 5. Student checks the actions available on the completed appointment | Verify that "Edit" is not available — the appointment is read-only |
| 6. Student opens the completed appointment detail | Verify that no editable fields or edit controls are present |

**Post-conditions:**
- Students can only edit appointments that are in "Pending" status
- Approved and completed appointments are read-only for students
- Students can still cancel approved appointments if needed
