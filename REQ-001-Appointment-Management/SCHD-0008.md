# SCHD-0008: Counselor views their appointments list

**Summary:** Verify that a counselor can view appointments assigned to them, showing student information and counselor-specific actions.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **counselor** account
- At least one student has booked an appointment with this counselor

**Scenario 1: Counselor views their assigned appointments**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Counselor navigates to the appointments page | Verify that the appointments list loads |
| 2. Verify appointment cards | Verify that each card shows the **student's** name/avatar, date, time, reason preview, and status badge |
| 3. Verify that only appointments assigned to this counselor are shown | Verify that no appointments belonging to other counselors appear |
| 4. Verify action buttons on a pending appointment card | Verify that "Accept" and "Reschedule" options are available in the dropdown menu |
