# GMET-0009: Counselor denies Google OAuth consent

**Summary:** Verify that when a counselor starts the Google connection flow but denies consent on the Google screen, they are returned to the dashboard with the connection not established.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **counselor** account
- The counselor has not previously connected a Google account
- The counselor is on the settings page

**Scenario 1: Deny Google consent during connection**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Counselor clicks "Connect Google Account" on the settings page | Verify that the browser redirects to Google's consent screen |
| 2. Counselor clicks "Cancel" or "Deny" on the Google consent screen instead of granting permission | Verify that Google redirects back to the app |
| 3. The counselor arrives back at the dashboard | Verify that no success message appears — instead, the Google section still shows "Connect Google Account" (not "Disconnect") |
| 4. Counselor navigates to the settings page | Verify that the Google integration still shows as not connected |
| 5. Counselor tries again and this time grants consent | Verify that the connection succeeds and the settings page now shows "Connected" |

**Post-conditions:**
- The counselor's Google account is **not** connected after denying consent
- No error message is shown to the counselor — the denial is handled gracefully
- The counselor can attempt to connect again at any time
