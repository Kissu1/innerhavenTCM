# SCHD-0021: Counselor reschedules a pending appointment

**Summary:** Verify that a counselor can reschedule a pending appointment to a new date and time.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **counselor** account
- A student has a **pending** appointment with this counselor

**Scenario 1: Reschedule to a new date and time**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Counselor navigates to the appointments page and finds a pending appointment | Verify that the appointment card shows a "Reschedule" action in the dropdown menu |
| 2. Counselor clicks "Reschedule" | Verify that an inline date/time picker or reschedule form appears |
| 3. Counselor picks a new future date from the calendar or date picker | Verify that the date is accepted |
| 4. Counselor selects a new available time slot | Verify that the time is accepted |
| 5. Counselor confirms the reschedule | Verify that a loading state appears |
| 6. The request completes | Verify that a success confirmation is displayed |
| 7. Counselor opens the appointment detail | Verify that the date and time reflect the new values entered in Steps 3–4 |
| 8. Verify the appointment status | Verify that the appointment remains in **"Pending"** status |

**Post-conditions:**
- The appointment date and time are updated
- The appointment status remains "Pending"
- The old time slot is freed and the new time slot is occupied
