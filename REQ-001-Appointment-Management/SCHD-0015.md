# SCHD-0015: Student cancels an approved appointment

**Summary:** Verify that a student can cancel an approved appointment.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **student** account
- The student has an **approved** appointment

**Scenario 1: Cancel an approved appointment**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Student navigates to the appointments page and finds an approved appointment | Verify that the appointment card shows a "Cancel" action in the dropdown menu |
| 2. Student clicks "Cancel" and confirms | Verify that the cancellation succeeds |
| 3. Verify the updated status | Verify that the appointment card's status badge changes to **"Cancelled"** |

**Post-conditions:**
- The appointment status is changed to "Cancelled"
- If the appointment was online, the Google Meet session is no longer accessible through the appointment
