# NOTI-0012: Student receives a booking rescheduled notification

**Summary:** Verify that when a counselor reschedules an appointment, the student receives a notification about the change.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **student** account
- The student has a pending appointment
- A counselor reschedules the student's pending appointment to a new date and time

**Scenario 1: Rescheduled notification**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. After the counselor reschedules, the student checks the notification bell or notifications page | Verify that a new notification appears with a message about the booking being updated (e.g., "Booking updated to {date} at {time}") |
| 2. Verify the notification type icon | Verify that the notification shows a Calendar icon (booking_rescheduled type) |
| 3. Student clicks the notification | Verify that the page navigates to the appointment detail page showing the updated date and time |

**Post-conditions:**
- The student is informed of the rescheduled appointment and can view the new details
