# NOTI-0007: Student sees an approved notification with Google Meet link

**Summary:** Verify that a booking_approved notification for an online appointment includes a clickable Google Meet link in the message.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **student** account
- A counselor has approved the student's online appointment, triggering a booking_approved notification

**Scenario 1: View approved notification with Meet link**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Student checks the notification bell or notifications page for the approval notification | Verify that the notification message includes text about the booking being approved |
| 2. Verify the message content | Verify that the message contains a "Join Meeting" link |
| 3. Student clicks the "Join Meeting" link | Verify that Google Meet opens in a new tab with the session ready to join |

**Post-conditions:**
- The student can join the online session directly from the notification
