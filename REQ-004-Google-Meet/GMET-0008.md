# GMET-0008: Student views Meet link on approved online appointment

**Summary:** Verify that after a counselor approves an online appointment with Google Meet, the student can see and access the Meet link from their appointment detail view.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **student** account
- The student has an online appointment that was approved by the counselor and a Google Meet link was created

**Scenario 1: Student views and accesses Meet link**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Student navigates to the appointments page | Verify that the approved online appointment is visible with status "Approved" and mode "online" |
| 2. Student clicks on the appointment card | Verify that the appointment detail page opens showing all details including the meeting link |
| 3. Inspect the meeting link display | Verify that the Google Meet link is displayed prominently (e.g., a "Join Google Meet" button or a clickable link) |
| 4. Student clicks the Meet link | Verify that the link opens in a new tab pointing to `https://meet.google.com/...` |
| 5. Verify the link is functional | Verify that clicking the link opens a valid Google Meet session where the student can join the call |

**Post-conditions:**
- The student can view and access the Google Meet link for their approved online appointment
- The link is valid and opens Google Meet in a new tab
