# GMET-0005: Temporary Meet creation failure — counselor can retry

**Summary:** Verify that when Google Meet is temporarily unavailable, the counselor sees a clear error message and can retry approving the appointment without needing to reconnect their Google account.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **counselor** account who has Google connected
- A pending **online** appointment exists
- Google Meet service is temporarily unavailable (e.g., rate limited or briefly down)

**Scenario 1: Meet creation fails temporarily — retry succeeds**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Counselor opens the pending online appointment and clicks "Approve" | Verify that an error message appears: "Unable to create Google Meet link right now. Please try again." |
| 2. Counselor checks the appointment status | Verify that the appointment is still "Pending" — it was not approved |
| 3. Counselor checks for any reconnection prompt | Verify that there is **no** prompt to reconnect Google — this is a temporary issue, not a token problem |
| 4. Counselor waits a moment and clicks "Approve" again | Verify that this time the approval succeeds, the Meet link appears, and the status changes to "Approved" |

**Post-conditions:**
- The appointment remains pending until a successful retry
- The counselor is not misled into reconnecting their Google account
- Once the temporary issue resolves, approval works normally
