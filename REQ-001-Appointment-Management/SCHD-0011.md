# SCHD-0011: Student edits a pending appointment (change date and time)

**Summary:** Verify that a student can change the date and time of a pending appointment.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **student** account
- The student has a **pending** appointment

**Scenario 1: Change date and time of a pending appointment**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Student clicks "Edit" on a pending appointment | Verify that the booking form loads pre-filled with current appointment data |
| 2. Student navigates the calendar to a different future date | Verify that the available time slots for the new date are loaded |
| 3. Student selects a different available time slot | Verify that the new slot is highlighted and the session summary sidebar updates with the new date and time |
| 4. Student clicks "Save Changes" | Verify that the submission succeeds |
| 5. Student opens the appointment detail | Verify that the date and time shown match the new selections from Steps 2–3 |

**Post-conditions:**
- The old time slot becomes available again for other students
- The new time slot is now occupied
