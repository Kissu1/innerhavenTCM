# SCHD-0024: Counselor declines a pending appointment

**Summary:** Verify that a counselor can decline a student's pending appointment request, and the student is notified that their request was not accepted.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **counselor** account
- A student has a **pending** appointment with this counselor

**Scenario 1: Counselor declines the booking request**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Counselor navigates to the appointments page and finds a pending appointment | Verify that the appointment card shows a "Decline" action in the dropdown menu alongside "Accept" |
| 2. Counselor clicks "Decline" | Verify that a confirmation prompt appears asking to confirm the decline action |
| 3. Counselor confirms the decline | Verify that a brief loading state appears, then the appointment status updates |
| 4. The appointment card updates | Verify that the status badge changes to **"Declined"** with an appropriate color (e.g., red or gray) |
| 5. Counselor opens the appointment detail | Verify that the status is shown as "Declined" and no Google Meet link was generated |
| 6. The student checks their appointments page | Verify that the appointment now shows status **"Declined"** — the student knows their request was not accepted |
| 7. The student checks their notifications | Verify that a notification was received indicating the booking was declined |

**Post-conditions:**
- The appointment status is "Declined"
- The time slot is freed and becomes available for other students to book
- Both the counselor and student see the declined status
- The student is notified of the decline
