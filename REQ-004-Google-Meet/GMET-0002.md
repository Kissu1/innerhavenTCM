# GMET-0002: Google Meet link appears when counselor approves online appointment

**Summary:** Verify that when a counselor approves a pending online appointment, a Google Meet link is automatically created and becomes visible to both the counselor and the student.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **counselor** account who has Google connected
- A pending **online** appointment exists for this counselor

**Scenario 1: Approve online appointment and see the Meet link**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Counselor opens the pending online appointment from their dashboard | Verify that the appointment shows mode "online" and a pending status |
| 2. Counselor clicks "Approve" | Verify that a brief loading indicator appears, then the appointment updates |
| 3. The appointment detail refreshes | Verify that a "Join Google Meet" link or button now appears on the appointment, and the status shows "Approved" |
| 4. Counselor returns to their appointments list | Verify that the appointment card now shows "Approved" and indicates it's an online session with a Meet link available |
| 5. The student logs in and opens the same appointment | Verify that the student also sees the "Join Google Meet" link and can click it to open Google Meet |

**Post-conditions:**
- The appointment is "Approved" with a working Google Meet link
- Both the counselor and the student can access the Meet link from their respective views
