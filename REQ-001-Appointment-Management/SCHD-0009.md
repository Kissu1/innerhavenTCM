# SCHD-0009: Student views appointment detail

**Summary:** Verify that a student can view the full details of a specific appointment, including meeting link for approved online sessions.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **student** account
- The student has at least one appointment (including at least one approved online appointment with a Google Meet link)

**Scenario 1: View appointment detail and meeting link**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Student clicks on an appointment card from the appointments list | Verify that the appointment detail page opens |
| 2. Verify the counselor section | Verify that the counselor's name and avatar are displayed |
| 3. Verify the meeting logistics section | Verify that the date, time range (e.g., "10:00 – 11:00"), and session mode ("Online" or "In-person") are shown |
| 4. Verify the concern/reason section | Verify that the full reason text entered during booking is displayed |
| 5. Verify the status display | Verify that the current status is shown with the appropriate badge color |
| 6. Student opens the detail of an approved online appointment | Verify that a Google Meet link or "Join Session Now" button is displayed |
| 7. Student clicks the meeting link or "Join" button | Verify that Google Meet opens in a new tab with the session ready to join |
