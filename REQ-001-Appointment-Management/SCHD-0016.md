# SCHD-0016: Counselor cancels an appointment

**Summary:** Verify that a counselor can cancel both pending and approved appointments assigned to them.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **counselor** account
- A student has a **pending** appointment with this counselor
- A student has an **approved** appointment with this counselor

**Scenario 1: Counselor cancels pending and approved appointments**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Counselor navigates to the appointments page and finds a pending appointment | Verify that the appointment card shows a "Cancel" action in the dropdown menu |
| 2. Counselor clicks "Cancel" and confirms | Verify that the cancellation succeeds |
| 3. Verify the updated status | Verify that the appointment card's status badge changes to **"Cancelled"** |
| 4. Counselor finds an approved appointment | Verify that the appointment card also shows a "Cancel" action |
| 5. Counselor clicks "Cancel" and confirms | Verify that the cancellation succeeds and the status changes to **"Cancelled"** |

**Post-conditions:**
- Both appointments have status "Cancelled"
- The time slots are freed and become available for booking
