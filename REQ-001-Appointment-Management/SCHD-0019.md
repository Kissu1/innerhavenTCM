# SCHD-0019: Counselor approves an online appointment without Google connection

**Summary:** Verify that approving an online appointment without a valid Google token shows a reconnect prompt, and that reconnecting restores approval capability.

**Preconditions:**
- User is logged in with a **counselor** account
- A student has a **pending** **online** appointment with this counselor
- The counselor has NOT connected their Google account or their Google token has expired

**Scenario 1: Approval fails without Google connection and reconnect restores it**

| Step | Expected Behavior |
|------|-------------------|
| 1. Counselor navigates to the appointments page and finds a pending online appointment | Verify that the appointment card shows an "Accept" action |
| 2. Counselor clicks "Accept" | Verify that a loading state appears |
| 3. The request completes | Verify that an error message is displayed indicating the Google connection has expired |
| 4. Verify the UI response | Verify that a "Reconnect Google" button or link is displayed |
| 5. Verify the appointment status | Verify that the appointment remains in **"Pending"** status (not approved) |
| 6. Counselor clicks the "Reconnect Google" button | Verify that the counselor is taken to the settings page where they can reconnect their Google account |
| 7. After reconnecting and returning to the appointments page | Verify that the counselor can now successfully approve the appointment |

**Post-conditions:**
- The appointment remains in "Pending" status until Google is reconnected
- No Google Meet link is generated until successful reconnection
